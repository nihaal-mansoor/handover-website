This page tracks registered property transactions in Dubai and is updated as new
data is published. It is a record, not a forecast.

Latest data runs to **3 September 2026**.

## Sales, year to date

Every year cut to 3 September, so the partial year compares fairly.

| Year | Sales | Total value | Median price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024 | 112,818 | AED 331.3bn | AED 1,550,000 |
| 2025 | 139,601 | AED 448.4bn | AED 1,600,000 |
| 2026 | 113,792 | AED 355.4bn | AED 1,422,450 |

2026 sits within a percent of 2024 on volume. 2025 stands out as the peak year rather
than 2026 standing out as a bad one.

## Off-plan share of sales

| Year | Off-plan |
| --- | --- |
| 2021 | 39.4% |
| 2022 | 44.4% |
| 2023 | 51.3% |
| 2024 | 60.2% |
| 2025 | 62.5% |
| 2026 | **68.5%** |

More than two thirds of registered sales are now off-plan. This is the single biggest
structural change in the dataset, and it pulls the median transaction price down
independently of what any completed property is worth.

## When people buy

Average monthly sales, 2022 to 2025:

| | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | May | Jun |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales | 8,909 | 9,590 | 10,747 | 9,622 | 11,800 | 10,930 |

| | Jul | Aug | Sep | Oct | Nov | Dec |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales | 12,238 | 12,525 | 13,741 | **13,744** | 12,547 | 12,308 |

October is the busiest month and January the quietest, a **54% difference**. If you
are buying rather than selling, the quiet months are where the negotiating room is.

## What this data is and is not

- **Registered transactions**, not asking prices. It is what actually completed, and
  it lags the market by the time registration takes.
- **Medians, Dubai-wide.** Your building is not the median.
- **A falling median is not a discount.** Price per square metre and median
  transaction price have moved in opposite directions during 2026, because the mix of
  what sells has changed.
- **2026 is partial**, to 3 September. Every comparison here is cut to the same date.

## Method

Dubai Land Department registered transactions, downloaded 4 September 2026:
1,771,170 records from 2000 to 3 September 2026, after excluding 5,760 rows with
impossible dates, one of them recorded in the year 1416.

Price-per-square-metre figures are residential only and clipped to 500–200,000 AED
per sqm. Figures are produced by a script kept with this site.

Updated as new data is published.
